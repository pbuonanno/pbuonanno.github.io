03 / Profile

About.

I am Professor of Economics and Director of the Department of Economics at the University of Bergamo. My research focuses on questions in the economics of crime, political economy, law and economics, social interactions and applied economic history, primarily using applied microeconometric methods.

I previously served as Vice-Rector for Scientific Research and Institutional Communication and as Director of Undergraduate Studies in Economics. This website collects my publications, ongoing research and current teaching activities.
