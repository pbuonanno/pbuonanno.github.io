03 / Profile

About.

I am Professor of Economics and Director of the Department of Economics at the University of Bergamo. My research combines applied microeconometric analysis with questions about institutions, criminal activity, social interactions and long-run development.

I previously served as Vice-Rector for Scientific Research and Institutional Communication and as Director of Undergraduate Studies in Economics.
